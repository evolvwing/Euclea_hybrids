# Statistical and plotting methods

B: cross-level egg counts (F1 n=3; incisa sib-cross n=5), arithmetic means ± SEM. Two-sided Welch t-test, unequal variances. Each point is a cross; no claim of specimen-level replication.

D: nonempty hatch-to-cocoon durations (58 F1, 10 sib-cross, 29 parental individuals). Welch ANOVA and Games–Howell pairwise comparisons across the three groups. All three pairwise comparisons are significant at 0.05 in the audit. Letters a/b/c denote this grouping; ordering of the letters is arbitrary.

C: separate Pearson chi-square goodness-of-fit tests against 1:1 male:female **adult** counts for each brood. Dead larvae and dead pupae are excluded from these tests and plotted in a separate stacked bar. Stars: * p<0.05, ** p<0.01, *** p<0.001; ns otherwise. These are six separate, unadjusted tests, including the two parental broods.

E/F: forewing lengths are analyzed separately by sex, using only rows whose wing_status is measured. Nonempty pooled-F1 × diapause-state and parental/control × diapause-state groups are compared by Welch ANOVA and Games–Howell pairwise tests. No letters on individual F1 broods. All 16 displayed letter-sharing relations agree with the recomputed Games–Howell decisions at 0.05. Shared letters indicate no detected difference, not equivalence. Donut percentages use all sexed individuals with Y/N diapause states, regardless of measurement status.

The audit scripts were reconstructed for this archive. Historical plotting scripts retained letters but did not retain the original test implementation; agreement with Games–Howell is independently verified here, not evidence of the original test's provenance. Pairwise raw Welch p-values are also supplied, not used as unadjusted multi-comparison significance claims.

Individuals can be clustered within broods. These descriptive individual-level duration and forewing comparisons do not account for shared brood environment, year effects, or family structure; the supplied duration data lack brood identifiers. They do not establish isolated causal diapause or genetic effects. Empty sex/diapause cells are displayed but not tested. The design is not a balanced factorial experiment.

C now includes parental adult counts: incisa 24 M/26 F (chi-square=0.08, p=0.7773); delphinii 11 M/14 F (chi-square=0.36, p=0.5485). No mortality counts were supplied for these broods; omitted mortality bars do not indicate zero mortality.
